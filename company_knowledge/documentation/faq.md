# Frequently asked questions

## What is Advanced Impactor?
[VERIFIED_PUBLIC] It is a cricket analysis and performance tool for players, parents, and coaches. Store descriptions list batting, bowling, ball tracking, biomechanics, and performance visualizations.

## What products belong to the company?
Public sources identify Advanced Impactor, CoachPlex, and Advanced Impactor Pro. Advanced Impactor focuses on cricket analysis. CoachPlex is listed for coaching management. Advanced Impactor Pro is described as a multi-sport Academy Management System.

## What are the four annotation labels?
[INTERNAL_CONTEXT] In supplied annotation context: 0 Random/noise, 1 Release, 2 Pitch, 3 Impact.

## How does the video pipeline work?
[HISTORICAL] The supplied architecture describes S3 video storage, a queue-based backend, Python rotation correction and ball-by-ball segmentation, and separate analysis workers. It is not independently confirmed as the current production flow.

## Is CoachPlex the same as Advanced Impactor Pro?
[UNVERIFIED] The relationship is not established in available sources.

## What is the model accuracy?
The available historical figures lack a defined metric and evaluation protocol. They should not be described as model accuracy.
