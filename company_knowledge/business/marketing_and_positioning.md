# Marketing and positioning

## Public product positioning
[VERIFIED_PUBLIC] App listings position Advanced Impactor as cricket analysis for players, parents, and coaches, designed to give objective information and support coaching. The Cricket Innovation Forum profile positions Advanced Impactor Pro as multi-sport academy management.

## Supplied company rationale
[INTERNAL_CONTEXT] The supplied brief describes goals of making analytics accessible, supporting measurable development, and connecting players, coaches, parents, and academies.

## Analytical observations
[ANALYTICAL] The descriptions suggest a two-part portfolio: on-field performance analysis and off-field coaching/academy operations. This is an interpretation, not a formal company statement. Avoid unsupported market-size, customer-count, or competitive claims.
