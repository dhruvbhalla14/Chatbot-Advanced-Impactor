# CoachPlex

## Publicly listed purpose
[VERIFIED_PUBLIC] The Google Play listing presents CoachPlex as a sports coaching management platform that connects coaches and athletes. It lists athlete rosters, schedules and session management, schedule-change requests, coaching observations, feedback, session history, fees, and earnings information.

## Supplied internal context
[INTERNAL_CONTEXT] The supplied company brief also associates Coach Plex with player, coach, and parent administration; academy admissions; fee management; training schedules; academy operations; and scouter-related operations. The exact current availability and role permissions for each item should be checked against current product documentation before promising it to a user.

## Product boundary
CoachPlex is described here as coaching and academy operations software. Do not conflate those administrative workflows with Advanced Impactor's cricket video analysis, ball tracking, or biomechanics features.
