# Advanced Impactor

## Product
[VERIFIED_PUBLIC] Advanced Impactor is presented in its current app listings as an AI-powered cricket analysis and performance tool for players, parents, and coaches. Users can record or upload cricket training video and review batting, bowling, biomechanics, ball movement, and performance insights.

## Listed capabilities
The public app descriptions list:
- Batting observations such as bat angles, foot positioning, shot selection, shot control, swing and movement patterns.
- Bowling analysis including line, length, speed, deviation, release mechanics, delivery patterns, and consistency.
- Ball tracking and 3D trajectory visualizations, including release, pitch, and impact points.
- Pitch maps, wagon wheels, frame-by-frame review, and performance history.
- Biomechanical observations about body positioning, movement sequencing, alignment, and technique.
- Video annotation features in version history, including offline video support, standalone annotation, and custom pitch zones.

These are descriptions published by the app developer in app store listings, not independent assessments of analytical validity or accuracy.

## Intended use and limits
The listings describe the tool as information to support training decisions and coaching conversations. They explicitly state that it does not replace a cricket coach. No model-accuracy or clinical biomechanical claims are established by these listings.
