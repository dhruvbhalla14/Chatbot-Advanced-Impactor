# Batting analysis

## Publicly listed capability
[VERIFIED_PUBLIC] The app listings describe batting insights that include bat angles, foot positioning, shot selection and control, swing and movement patterns, and impact-related metrics. The Rediff interview also reports batting observations such as footwork, head position, shot classification, biomechanics, and 3D angles.

## Limits
These are product descriptions and reported capabilities. The sources do not provide validated thresholds or independent accuracy measurements for each metric.
