# Biomechanics

## Publicly listed capability
[VERIFIED_PUBLIC] The public product descriptions list observations about body positioning, movement sequencing, alignment, and technique. For batting they mention swing path, torso rotation, and impact-chain timing. For bowling they mention stride flow, shoulder alignment, movement sequencing, and release mechanics.

## Limits
The app listings do not call this medical diagnosis or provide a validated biomechanical assessment protocol. Describe it as sports technique analysis and performance information, not medical advice.
