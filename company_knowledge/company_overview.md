# Advanced Impactor

## Company context
[INTERNAL_CONTEXT] Advanced Impactor is described in the supplied company brief as an AI-powered sports technology company focused on sports performance intelligence, particularly cricket analytics. Its stated objective is to make coaching and athlete development more measurable through video analysis, computer vision, AI, and performance analytics. The intended ecosystem connects players, coaches, parents, academies, and sports performance data.

The supplied company objectives are to convert recorded sports video into useful performance insights; provide measurable batting and bowling analytics; help coaches evaluate technique; make advanced analytics more accessible; and support player development through data-informed coaching. These are supplied internal context, not independently audited corporate mission statements.

## Public product description
[VERIFIED_PUBLIC] Google Play and Apple's App Store describe Advanced Impactor as a cricket video analysis and performance tool for players, parents, and coaches. Publicly listed capabilities include batting and bowling analysis, ball tracking, biomechanics, frame-by-frame review, performance visualizations, and reports. The listings say the product is intended to support coaches rather than replace coaching judgment.
